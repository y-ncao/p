# Listing Description

Charming and move-in ready, this beautifully maintained single-family home is filled with natural light and thoughtful updates. The modern kitchen brings together custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast area, and a skylight, while the living and family spaces create a warm flow for everyday life and entertaining.

The spacious family room features designer built-ins, a large skylight, and a stylish electric fireplace. Outside, the low-maintenance backyard combines hardscape and lawn for relaxed California indoor-outdoor living. With wood-style flooring, double-pane windows, central heating, central A/C, and an attached 2-car garage, the home presents a polished West San Jose lifestyle.
